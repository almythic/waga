Before you begin.

Detox Box by WAGA is a structured, food-based program. It is safe for most medical conditions. If you have any concerns, our nutritionist is available for individual consultations before you begin.

The program runs once a month. Exact dates will be announced on our website each month, and you will be able to sign up for that particular month's cycle.

We deliver across the NYC Tri-State Area:

New York — New York City, Long Island (Nassau & Suffolk), Lower Hudson Valley (Westchester, Rockland, Putnam)

New Jersey — North & Central NJ (Bergen, Hudson, Essex, Union, Middlesex)

Connecticut — Southwestern CT / Fairfield County (Stamford, Greenwich, Norwalk)

You can list allergies and key restrictions in the health profile section of the order form. We will review them and let you know whether we can accommodate your needs for the upcoming round.

This program is for educational and lifestyle support only and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Your spot is confirmed only after our team reviews your information, confirms availability, and payment is received.
