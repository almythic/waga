What Is This

More Than
a Meal Plan

A clinical protocol, made personal.

The WAGA five-day program is a structured nutritional reset designed by a team of doctors, nutritionists, and medical specialists. It originated as a hospitalized detox protocol in clinical settings — controlled, measured, supervised.

Over nearly twenty years, we refined it. First in hotel wellness retreats across Serbia, Montenegro, and Croatia. Then, during the pandemic, we adapted it for home delivery — without losing the medical rigor. Thousands of clients later, it arrives in New York.

Day One

Prepare

Your body begins to listen.

The first day is about easing in. Your metabolism starts to shift as processed inputs fall away and whole, calibrated nutrition takes over. You may feel a subtle awareness — a quiet signal that something new has begun.

Body

Digestive system begins to reset. Glycogen stores start to deplete as your body transitions to cleaner fuel sources. Hydration increases.

Mind

Initial adjustment. You may notice habitual cravings surface — this is awareness, not deprivation. Mental clarity begins at the edges.

Well-being

A sense of intention settles in. The act of beginning — of choosing this — creates its own momentum. Most clients report feeling purposeful.

2

Day Two

Deepen

The body finds its rhythm.

Your system adjusts. The initial novelty settles into a rhythm. Meals arrive, you eat mindfully, and between meals the body begins its quieter work — processing, releasing, recalibrating.

Body

Liver and kidney function optimizes for detoxification. Inflammatory markers begin to decrease. Sleep quality often improves noticeably.

Mind

Cravings diminish. Focus sharpens as blood sugar stabilizes. The mental noise of food decisions disappears — everything is decided for you.

Well-being

A growing sense of lightness. Clients often describe feeling "unburdened" — not just physically, but from the cognitive load of daily food choices.

3

Day Three

Reset

The turning point.

The midpoint. This is where the deepest work happens. Your metabolism has fully shifted. The body is in active restoration mode — every system recalibrating to a cleaner baseline. Many clients call this the day they "feel the difference."

Body

Peak detoxification activity. Cellular autophagy increases. Gut microbiome begins rebalancing. Skin often shows early signs of improvement.

Mind

Mental clarity peaks. Many clients report a sense of heightened perception — colors brighter, thoughts clearer, decisions easier.

Well-being

A shift from "doing a program" to "being in a process." The commitment deepens. There is quiet pride in having reached the center.

4

Day Four

Restore

Building on the new foundation.

Your body begins to build on the reset. The deepest detox is behind you; now it is about consolidation. Nutrients absorb more efficiently. Energy returns — not the jittery, caffeine kind, but something steadier and deeper.

Body

Nutrient absorption efficiency increases. Energy metabolism stabilizes at a new baseline. Inflammation continues to decrease. Digestion feels effortless.

Mind

A calm confidence emerges. The anxiety around food — what to eat, when, how much — has been replaced by trust in the process.

Well-being

Clients often describe this as the day they feel most "themselves." Not depleted, not striving — simply present and clear.

5

Day Five

Emerge

You feel the difference.

The final day. Your body has completed the arc. What you feel now — the clarity, the lightness, the quiet energy — is your new baseline. Not a temporary high, but a recalibration. The program ends, but the reset stays with you.

Body

Metabolic reset complete. Digestive efficiency at its highest. Skin clearer, sleep deeper, inflammation at its lowest point. Your body remembers what it feels like to be clean.

Mind

Sustained mental clarity. The fog is gone. Decision-making feels lighter. Many clients report carrying this sharpness for weeks after the program ends.

Well-being

A sense of accomplishment — quiet, not loud. You chose something difficult and stayed with it. That changes how you see yourself.
