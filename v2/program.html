What Is This

Detoxification &
Biotransformation

A fundamental biological process embedded in our evolution.

Every organism is equipped with its own system for processing and eliminating toxins.
While the liver is the central organ, detoxification is a networked process involving the intestines, kidneys, and lungs.
What truly differentiates us is not just exposure —
but our individual capacity to process and eliminate toxic load.

That capacity is dynamic.
It can be supported, optimized, and elevated —
when physiology is properly understood.

At the core of this system:
Phase I Detoxification — activation through the Cytochrome P450 system
Phase II Detoxification — a highly coordinated network of conjugation pathways
(glucuronidation, sulfation, methylation, acetylation, glutathione conjugation, and amino acid conjugation)

Without precise support, this system may become inefficient —
or even increase internal burden.
This is why detox should never be based on restriction.
It should be based on precision.
Not deprivation — but intelligent selection.
Not starvation — but structured, physiology-driven support.
