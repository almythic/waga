Where It Began

A Nutritionist Who Saw the Gap

Between clinical precision and everyday life.

Nadja Radonjić spent years working inside hospital detox programs — environments where every meal was measured, every outcome tracked, and every patient monitored by a medical team. The results were remarkable. But they ended at the hospital door.

Patients would return to their lives and, within weeks, lose everything they had gained. The problem was never willpower. It was infrastructure. There was no bridge between clinical-grade nutrition and the reality of a busy life. Nadja set out to build one.

The Journey

From Hospital to Home

Every step brought us closer to you.

The Beginning

Clinical Origins

Where science met nutrition.

The program was born inside hospital walls — a supervised detoxification protocol designed by doctors and nutritionists for patients who needed metabolic reset under medical care. Controlled, measured, effective.

The Expansion

Hotel Wellness Retreats

Clinical rigor, beautiful setting.

The program moved into hotel wellness centers across Serbia and the region. Clients could experience the full five-day protocol in a retreat setting — with the same medical oversight, but surrounded by comfort instead of clinical walls.

2020

Home Delivery

The world changed. We adapted.

When the pandemic made retreats impossible, the team engineered a home delivery system that preserved the nutritional precision and daily structure of the original protocol. Thousands of clients across four countries proved it worked.

2026

New York City

Nearly twenty years in the making.

WAGA Wellness brings the full program to Manhattan — the same medical foundation, the same team oversight, the same five-day arc. Adapted for New York. Delivered to your door.
